# Barlow and Barlow Condensed (font pack, v1)

- Files: static Regular, Medium, SemiBold and Bold of Barlow and Barlow Condensed, copied unchanged from google/fonts (ofl/barlow and ofl/barlowcondensed, on the main branch as of this copy). Checksums are in manifest.json and match the Slopographic font pack list.
